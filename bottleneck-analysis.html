Bottleneck #1 - 552K Audience, Nothing They Can Buy From You

The asymmetry: Bank of England, Global Fraud Summit, Agility PR Top 10 UK Finance Influencers, 552K combined social, HSBC + Experian + XTB sponsorships. Every prerequisite for a paid offer exists. The offer doesn't. Every UK Gen Z follower who finishes a TikTok and wants the next step lands on beacons.ai/cazzatime - which routes to more free TikToks or to a sponsor partner. They can't pay you for anything.

A productised ladder sized honestly to a Gen Z audience - £47-£97 self-paced "Money School" course + £197-£497 6-week "Cazza Time Cohort" - gives a 22-year-old learning their first ISA somewhere to spend money with you. High-ticket mentorship at £1.5K-£3K can sit above the cohort later as an upmarket line for landlords / freelancers / six-figure earners, but it is not the right entry door for the bulk of the audience.

Why it's costing you: - 0.3-0.6% of a 552K audience converts to a £47-£97 course at the right offer = 1,500-3,300 buyers x £47-£97 = £70-320K floor in Year-1 course revenue - Cohort tiers carry higher LTV - 60-150 paying mentees per intake at £197-£497 = £12-75K per cohort, multiple cohorts per year - Currently the Bank of England + Global Fraud Summit credibility is sitting on a brand that has nothing to sell. The credibility decays if it never gets attached to a product.

The fix: - "Money School" - 6-module self-paced video course at £47-£97. Covers budgeting, ISA stack, tax basics, side-hustle tax, first investment account, fraud awareness. - "Cazza Time Cohort" - 6-week live cohort at £197-£497. Weekly group Q&A with Cameron, action plans, capped intakes (50-100 mentees) to keep live element real. - Both fit FCA "education and information" boundaries cleanly. No regulated advice. Disclaimer in copy.

Estimated impact: Year-1 floor £100-280K from course + cohort combined. Floor scales with audience growth, brand collaborations, and post-course upsell into community / newsletter sponsorship.

Why this is #1: Highest ceiling. The full credibility stack (Bank of England + Global Fraud Summit + Top 10 UK Finance Influencers) is the moat. Each month of audience growth without a product to point at compounds the missed revenue and locks in the "Cazza is free content only" frame, making future conversion harder.

Bottleneck #2 - 552K Audience Lives On TikTok And Meta - You Don't Own A Single Email

The leak: beacons.ai/cazzatime captures zero email addresses. The 552K combined IG + TikTok audience has nowhere to opt in. Your entire reach is rented from Adam Mosseri and ByteDance. If TikTok had a UK ban tomorrow (the conversation has not gone away), 434K of the 552K reach disappears overnight. The brand survives on reach you do not own.

An "ISA + Tax Allowance Audit" scorecard - 12 questions, returns a 0-100 score, segments by life stage (student / first job / first home / first investor / first landlord), captures email - converts the casual TikTok viewer into a tracked email subscriber Cazza can market to for life. Same scorecard pulls double duty as a Lifetime ISA / cash ISA / stocks-and-shares ISA decision tool, which is content you already make for free.

Why it's costing you: - 5-9% TikTok-to-email is normal for UK finance creators with the right magnet = 25-50K incremental email subscribers from existing audience base - Without an owned list, you cannot pre-sell course launches, cohort intakes, or sponsor activations to a warm audience - you start cold every time - Comparable creators (Damien Talks Money, Mr MoneyJar, Mike Thompson) run scorecards and newsletters on the same UK Gen Z money audience and capture email + first product touch. Each week without your scorecard, your warm audience trickles to creators who set up the lead-capture you didn't.

The fix: - "ISA + Tax Allowance Audit" - 12-question quiz on ScoreApp or Typeform - Captures email, returns a custom action plan based on score band - Segments by: ISA usage, debt position, investing readiness, life stage, tax-allowance utilisation - Triggers an 8-touch nurture sequence routed by segment (course-buyer pool vs cohort-ready vs newsletter-only) - Newsletter on Beehiiv or Substack - tied to scorecard signup, 2x weekly cadence, monetised on its own (sponsorships + paid tier later)

Estimated impact: Year-1 lift £35-90K direct from email-driven course / cohort sales. Newsletter monetisation (£0 in Year-1 ramp, £20-60K in Year-2 ARR) compounds for life of the list.

Why this is #2: Foundational for #1 and #3. Fast install (under 7 days). Compounds infinitely - the email list is the asset every other layer rides on. A TikTok ban risk alone justifies the install on its own.

Bottleneck #3 - Sponsorship Income Caps At Brands' Marketing Budgets

The contradiction: HSBC UK, Experian UK, XTB pay you per post. Each sponsor pays once, the audience watches once, the relationship resets to zero. The audience is the asset; sponsors are renting it from you, one post at a time. Sponsor revenue is real revenue but it has a ceiling shaped by other companies' marketing calendars - not by how big your audience grows.

A "Cazza Members" community at £6-£12/mo (or £67-£127/yr) gives the audience a recurring layer to anchor to. Members get a private monthly Q&A, a sponsor-product breakdown digest (the analyst version of your sponsor work, where you walk through how the product actually compares to alternatives), early-access cohort priority, and a UK Gen Z money community.

Why it's costing you: - 552K audience x even 0.3% converting to £8/mo = ~£14-19K/mo recurring = £170-230K/yr ARR layer that does not exist today - Recurring revenue smooths the sponsorship cycle - you stop being one missed quarter from a cash crunch - Members produce the highest LTV cohort applicants (warm, engaged, already paying for the relationship) - The sponsor-product breakdown angle adds an editorial layer that protects the brand from "Cazza only talks up sponsors" perception drift, because members get the unvarnished version

The fix: - "Cazza Members" - £8/mo (or £87/yr with a small discount) recurring community - Hosted on Skool or Circle (low-overhead moderation) - Monthly live Q&A with Cameron (60 min, recorded for the vault) - Sponsor-product breakdown digest (monthly written analyst piece) - Early-access cohort priority + member-only AMA threads - Auto-included as a 3-month trial with course or cohort purchase

Estimated impact: Year-1 floor £80-180K ARR. Compounds with every new follower wave, every Bank of England moment, every cohort cycle.

Why this is #3: Smaller ceiling than #1, depends on Bottleneck #2 for nurture infrastructure, but locks in audience LTV that compounds for life of the brand. Once a member is in for 3 months, retention runs at industry-standard 65-75% which means the recurring revenue holds even if a quarter of TikTok content underperforms.
