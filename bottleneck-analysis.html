Three bottlenecks I found inside your funnel

Studied: beacons.ai/cazzatime · TikTok @cazzatime · IG @cazza_time · YouTube channel · Mr MoneyJar Show EP 104 · Bank of England + Global Fraud Summit appearances · LinkedIn (uk.linkedin.com/in/cameron-cazzatime-smith).

Three bottlenecks ranked by revenue impact. Year-1 floor numbers below are conservative ranges built off comparable UK creator-mentorship benchmarks (Damien Talks Money, Mike Thompson, Toby Newbatch). Engineering brain assumed - every number is defensible.

01 · Highest Revenue Lever - 553K Audience, Nothing They Can Buy

beacons.ai/cazzatime is the entire owned-funnel layer. It routes to TikTok, IG, YouTube, and sponsor links. There is no Cazza-owned product on it. Every UK Gen Z follower who finishes a TikTok and wants the next step has nowhere to go. They watch. They learn. They scroll.

The Bank of England guest spot, the Global Fraud Summit speaker slot, the No10 / Treasury invites - all of it is sitting on a brand that has nothing to sell. Your credibility stack would carry a £1.5K-£4K mentorship offer the day it launched.

Cost of waiting: ~£12-32K/mo in cohort revenue going to no one. Compounds to ~£150-400K/yr in unbuilt mentorship revenue while the audience grows past 600K. Each month an audience member discovers Cazza, follows for a week, and never sees a Cazza-owned product is a permanent miss - they install the habit of treating you as free content, not a teacher they can pay.

6 months from now: ~£75-200K never collected. Plus an audience now 100K+ larger that has never been offered anything - they're harder to convert later because the "Cazza is free content only" frame is locked in.

Fix: Gen Z Finance Operator Mentorship - 8-week cohort, £1.5K-£4K, 60-150 paying mentees per intake, weekly Zoom with Cazza, content + ISA + investing + sponsor-to-product playbook. Year-1 floor: £150-400K direct + post-cohort waitlist nurture.

02 · Fast Install - 553K Audience, Zero Email Capture

The 553K combined IG + TikTok audience has nowhere to opt in. There's no newsletter on beacons.ai/cazzatime, no quiz, no score, no email magnet. Every follower lives on TikTok / Meta - which means the entire audience is rented from Adam Mosseri and ByteDance.

If TikTok had a UK ban tomorrow (the conversation hasn't gone away), 434K of the 553K reach disappears overnight. The brand survives on reach you don't own.

Meanwhile Damien Talks Money, Mr MoneyJar, and Mike Thompson are running quizzes and newsletters on the same UK Gen Z money audience and capturing email + first product touch. Every week without your quiz = your warm audience trickling to creators who set up the lead-capture you didn't.

Cost of waiting: ~£3-9K/mo in nurture-driven mentorship + sponsor-segment revenue not happening. ~£35-110K/yr compounding for life of the list you don't have. 30-50K of your existing audience could be on it right now. They're not, because there's no opt-in.

6 months from now: ~£18-54K never captured + an extra ~15-30K warm followers permanently routed to competitor newsletters. The list keeps shrinking against the version of itself you should already have.

Fix: "UK Money Score" quiz on ScoreApp / Typeform. 12 questions, returns score 0-100, segments by ISA usage / debt / investing readiness. 30-50K incremental email subscribers from existing audience. £35-110K Year-1 lift, compounds for life of the list.

03 · Compound Pickup - Sponsorships Extract Value Once, Audience Stays Un-Monetised

HSBC UK and the other brand-sponsorship work is good revenue today. It also has a ceiling. Each sponsor pays you once for the post, the audience watches it once, and the relationship resets to zero. The audience is the asset. Sponsors are renting it from you, one post at a time.

There's no recurring layer. No paid community, no monthly Q&A vault, no segmented sponsor-product matchmaking that would let the audience pay you for ongoing access between cohort intakes. Your Bank of England + Global Fraud Summit credibility could carry a £19/mo "Cazza Members" community at 1-2% conversion of the IG audience alone.

Cost of waiting: ~£2-6K/mo in recurring revenue never collected. The £25-72K/yr ARR layer keeps not getting built each cycle. Every month another cohort of new followers cools without ever being given a Cazza-owned product to anchor to.

6 months from now: Another six months of an audience that grew 100K+ and was never given a way to pay you for anything between sponsor posts. The recurring layer that should already be live is still not live.

Fix: "Cazza Members" £19/mo community. Monthly live Q&A, sponsor-product matchmaking digest (so members get the analyst version of your sponsor work, not just the ad version), early-access cohort priority, private UK Gen Z money community. £25-72K/yr ARR at 1-2% conversion of IG audience. Compounds with every cohort and every Bank of England moment that drives a follower spike.
