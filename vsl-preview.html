We don't get taught the difference between Plan 1 and Plan 2 student loans, even though picking the wrong strategy costs most graduates tens of thousands across their working life. Six months out from my first salary I started teaching myself the lessons school forgot. Then I started teaching everyone else."

Credibility (0:25 - 0:40)

"434 thousand of you on TikTok. 118 thousand on Instagram. The Bank of England invited me in to talk about interest rates. The Global Fraud Summit invited me to speak alongside governments and tech platforms about how fraud is hitting Gen Z online. Treasury and No10 keep inviting me to roundtables. None of that was the goal when I started. It became the goal because the audience showed up."

"For three years I've answered the same questions in your DMs and your comments. ISA. SIPP. Student loans. First job. First payslip. The full UK money operating system."

Production notes

  • Camera: Phone front-camera or single mirrorless on a tripod. Match the IG / TikTok feel exactly. Do not look like a corporate webinar.
  • Background: Plain warm wall, soft natural window light from camera-left. The Bank of England press credentials can sit on the desk in the lower frame as an Easter-egg trust signal.
  • Audio: Lavalier mic. The numbers in the script need to land - audio quality matters more than camera.
  • Cuts: 4-6 cuts max. Each cut held 12-25 seconds. Do not over-edit - the voice is plain, the cuts should match.
  • Wardrobe: Cazza's existing on-camera wardrobe (clean t-shirt or soft hoodie, IG-aesthetic). No suit, no tie. The brand is anti-corporate-finance.
  • Text overlays: Sparingly. Numbers only ("434K", "118K", "Bank of England", "Global Fraud Summit", "8 weeks", "150 seats"). No emojis on the landing-page asset.
  • Length: 90 seconds. Anything longer and the audience swipes away.
  • CTA on screen: Last 10 seconds, "Apply below" lower-third, plus a soft animated arrow pointing at the apply CTA.
