Foundation

Copy Strategy - Cameron "Cazza" Smith

Built: 2026-05-02 Built for: @cazza_time

Voice

  • Plain-English, no City-trader bravado, no jargon-flex
  • Slightly self-deprecating ("I had a First-Class Engineering Masters and still couldn't read my first payslip")
  • Engineer brain - numbers must check out, ranges given as floors not ceilings
  • UK-native vocabulary - ISA, SIPP, LISA, NI, tax code, plan 1/2/4/5 student loan, "uni" not "college"
  • Anti-guru. Pro-teacher.
  • Conversational register. Reads like a TikTok caption stretched into a paragraph, not a Wall Street whitepaper.

Use: ISA, LISA, SIPP, allowance, payslip, tax code, plain-English, taught, learned, system, the maths, walk you through, on your side, no jargon Avoid: game-changer, transform, unlock, level up, hustle, secrets, hacks, blueprint, supercharge, abundance, manifest, quantum leap, journey, vibe, disrupt, pivot, get rich, make millions, alpha

Brand pillars (course + cohort landing copy hangs on these)

  1. Built by an engineer who learned the hard way. First-Class Masters in Electrical Engineering. Got his first payslip and could not read it. Started teaching himself the UK money system. Started teaching everyone else.
  2. UK-native by design. ISAs, NI, tax codes, student loans, LISAs. The money lessons school in the UK forgot. No US copy-paste content.
  3. Trusted by the institutions. Bank of England content collab. UK Government Global Fraud Summit attendee. The same Cazza explaining your ISA on TikTok is the Cazza the Bank of England put on camera with their interest-rate economists.
  4. Honest pricing for honest learners. Self-paced course at £47-£97 for the foundation. Six-week cohort at £197-£497 for the people who want guided. No £2K+ "mentorship" upsell baked into the front door, because most of the audience are 22 and learning their first ISA.

Sub-CTA microcopy

  • "Enrol in Money School"
  • "Reserve a cohort seat"
  • "Skip the setter call - apply direct"
  • "Join the next intake"
  • "Cohort caps at 100"

Body copy register

  • Short paragraphs. 2-4 sentences max.
  • One number per claim.
  • Ranges given as floors. "From X to Y" not "up to Z."
  • Personal pronouns. "You" addressing one specific UK Gen Z reader. "I" when Cazza speaks.
  • Zero em dashes (use commas, full stops, parentheses, or " - " instead).
  • Zero AI-slop vocabulary - copy runs through the AG1 validator before deploy.

FCA boundary

  • "Education and information" framing only. No regulated financial advice.
  • "Here is how an ISA works" not "buy this fund."
  • Disclaimer: "Cazza Time is an educational brand. Nothing on this page is regulated financial advice. For regulated advice, speak with an FCA-authorised advisor."

Avoid

  • "Get rich quick" / "make millions" - Bank of England credibility forbids it
  • US-finance vocabulary (401k, Roth, FICO)
  • Crypto-pump tone
  • Setter-call sales pages - Cazza's audience is Gen Z, prefers self-serve
  • Long-form pitch decks - the brand is short-form, the funnel must respect that
  • Anything that frames sponsorships as "selling out" - they are a layer of the stack, not a flaw
  • High-ticket-only positioning - Gen Z audience needs the £47 door, not just the £497 door

What to do instead

  • Use UK money vocabulary fluently
  • Quote real ISA / LISA / NI numbers
  • Show the Bank of England + Global Fraud Summit credibility once, then let the offer carry the weight
  • Keep the apply-to-enrol flow under 4 minutes
  • Apply form is the CTA - no calendar, no setter
  • Show the cohort cap explicitly ("we open intakes quarterly, cap at 100")
  • Lead with the course at £47-£97 as the primary entry product. Cohort at £197-£497 sits next to it for the audience segment that wants live.

Tone calibration by surface

Surface Register Length
TikTok / IG hook Direct, specific to a UK money pain 1 line
Email subject Question or curiosity, never clickbait 4-8 words
Hero VSL opener "I had a Masters in engineering. I still couldn't read my payslip." 1 sentence cold open
Landing hero What's at stake for a UK 22-year-old learning their first ISA 1 promise + 1 mechanism
FAQ Plain answers, no hedge, FCA-clean 2-3 sentences each

Voice test

Every paragraph passes if a 23-year-old graduate in the UK reading it nods, not laughs. If it could appear in a generic "scale your business" course or a US-finance creator's funnel, rewrite it. The voice has to sound like the same person who explained ISAs in 60 seconds on TikTok - just with more room.
