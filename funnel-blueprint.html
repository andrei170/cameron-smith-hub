Phase mapping

Phase One - Days 1-3 - Scorecard + Email Capture Live

  • "ISA + Tax Allowance Audit" scorecard built on ScoreApp / Typeform (12 questions, returns 0-100, segments by ISA usage / debt / investing readiness / life stage)
  • beacons.ai/cazzatime updated, scorecard becomes the primary CTA above sponsor links
  • Scorecard embed live on a Cazza-owned page (cazzatime.co.uk/score or similar)
  • Email capture wired to Beehiiv or ConvertKit
  • 4-touch scorecard-result nurture sequence segmented by score band (0-30 = Money School, 31-65 = course or cohort decision, 66+ = cohort-ready)

Phase Two - Days 4-7 - Course + Cohort Landing Live

  • Money School course landing page deployed (£47 standard, £97 with private Q&A access)
  • Cazza Time Cohort landing page deployed (this build = sample), £197-£497 with cohort cap of 100
  • Hero VSL embedded (Mr MoneyJar EP 104 as Day-1 placeholder, swap to custom 4-min Cazza VSL on Day 7)
  • Typeform application live for cohort (4-minute form, direct enrolment on submit)
  • Stripe + payment auto-trigger on enrolment for both course and cohort
  • Pre-cohort welcome flow (3 touches) + cohort kickoff sequence
  • Brand colours locked to Cazza's palette (Day 1 fix, could not extract cleanly from beacons)
  • FCA "education and information" disclaimer locked into footer

Phase Three - Post-install - Cazza Members + Recurring

  • £8/mo Cazza Members private community (Skool or Circle)
  • Monthly Q&A, sponsor-product breakdown digest, early-access cohort priority
  • Welcome flow + retention sequence
  • Cross-promotion in cohort (cohort grads default into 90-day free Members trial)

Phase Four - Post-install - Paid Traffic

  • Pixel + GA4 + retargeting tags installed and firing
  • Top-performing organic TikToks repurposed as Meta + TikTok ads
  • Retargeting campaigns for landing-page abandons + scorecard starts who did not complete
  • Cold campaigns to UK Gen Z lookalikes of course buyers

Integration points

  • TikTok / IG bio link: beacons.ai/cazzatime updated to push scorecard first, sponsors second
  • Scorecard to email: ScoreApp / Typeform webhook -> Beehiiv tag by score band + life stage
  • Email to course / cohort landing: scorecard-result nurture ends with the right CTA per score band (course for 0-30, course-or-cohort for 31-65, cohort for 66+)
  • Cohort landing to Stripe: Typeform application submit -> Stripe Checkout with cohort SKU
  • Course landing to Stripe: Stripe Checkout direct on Money School page (no application required)
  • Stripe to Skool: webhook auto-adds buyer to course or cohort space
  • Members £8/mo: Stripe subscription -> Skool Members tier
  • Pixel coverage: scorecard start, scorecard complete, course / cohort landing view, application start, application complete, Stripe purchase, Members signup

Post-install scaling

  • Course (Money School) is always-on at £47-£97, no intake limits
  • Cohort cadence: quarterly intakes, capped at 100 (waitlist nurture between)
  • Cazza Members: launches Day 30 post-cohort 1 close at £8/mo
  • Paid traffic: Day 60 onward, after scorecard + course + cohort organic conversion baseline established
  • Sponsorship layer: untouched. The course / cohort / Members ladder sits above sponsor work; sponsors continue at current run-rate
  • Newsletter monetisation: tested in Year-2 once list is at 30K+ subscribers
