YouTube long-form layer

YouTube is the underused asset. TikTok / IG do the reach, YouTube can do the depth.

The thesis: Long-form (15-25 min) UK Gen Z money education that lives forever, ranks for SEO, and feeds the cohort intake from search.

Format: - 1 video / week - Title format: "[Specific number / question] - Cazza Time UK Money" - Hook in first 8 seconds, then framework, then example, then CTA to quiz / cohort waitlist

First 6 videos to film:

  1. "The full UK ISA breakdown - which one you should open first"
  2. "Plan 1, 2, 4, 5 - which UK student loan you have and what it actually costs"
  3. "The first 3 UK accounts every Gen Z professional should have"
  4. "SIPP + employer match - the £100K mistake most UK Gen Z make"
  5. "I went to the Bank of England. Here's what I asked them about interest rates."
  6. "How I'd save my first £10K if I was 22 in the UK today"

Photoshoot brief (landing-page assets)

For the landing page, two asset types needed:

  1. Hero / VSL placeholder - Cazza headshot, professional but Gen Z, ideally wearing a softly branded hoodie or smart-casual shirt. Bright, warm, looks like the TikTok feed not a corporate brochure.
  2. About-section photo - Cazza in a "behind the scenes" shot - on a phone filming a TikTok, at the laptop, at a desk with Bank of England press credentials visible. Shows the work, not the polish.

Look + feel reference: Cazza's existing IG aesthetic - bright, clean, slightly warm tone, white-balanced. Avoid grey corporate-finance backdrops.

IG / TikTok roadmap (cohort intake window)

A 4-week priming runway before each cohort intake opens.

Week 1 - Mentorship-existence reveal

  • 3 TikToks teasing "the thing I've been building"
  • 2 IG posts on the cohort idea, why now, who it's for

Week 2 - Audience-specific value

  • 5 hook-bank TikToks (mix of money-school-forgot + ISA-stack)
  • 1 IG carousel - "What's inside the cohort, week by week"
  • 1 long-form YouTube video that ends with the cohort waitlist CTA

Week 3 - Application opens

  • 3 TikToks announcing the cohort intake (capped at 150, quarterly cadence)
  • IG bio + beacons.ai CTA swaps to the application page
  • 1 IG live walking through the application + answering questions

Week 4 - Final-call window

  • Daily TikTok counting down to cohort close
  • IG story polls + countdown stickers
  • Final 24-hour push email + IG story

After cohort closes, cadence drops back to standard sponsor + education. Members and post-cohort grads get separate retention content.
